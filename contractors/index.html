01 The thing nobody says

Replacement is the only thing most roofers sell

So you wait for a roof to die, then bid against every roofer in the county for the same job.

Everyone whose roof is aging but still has years left never calls you, because there’s nothing for them to buy from a roofer. That’s most of the roofs in your market.

Illustration

Every aging roof you walk away from is a job you didn’t have a product for.

Whatever your trade, the roof that’s aging but sound is the one you can’t sell anything to.

If you replace roofs

You tell the homeowner to call back in a few years, and hope they remember you when the roof finally goes.

If you do repairs and maintenance

You fix the flashing and leave a roof that keeps aging, and the next call might go to someone else.

If you soft wash or clean gutters

You clean the roof and walk away with nothing to offer that protects it.

If you install solar

An aging roof stalls the sale, or you install over shingles that may need replacing under the panels.

02 See the difference

Inside-Out Encapsulation

Coatings sit on the granules as a film that cracks and lifts. Rejuvenators soak in and leave nothing on top. NANOROOF’s professional-grade silicone does both jobs: it bonds inside the shingle and encapsulates it.

Real job

Two hands on a 3-ft wand, 240–500 psi at the tip (under 300 on most homes), 6–12 inches from the surface, 50% overlap, four-pass cross-hatch.

02 Proof, not promises

Most roof treatments ask homeowners to trust them. You’ll prove it.

Every NANOROOF job is measured as it goes on and documented when it’s done, so the warranty rests on evidence.

  • Wet-film readings: logged during the spray, so coverage is confirmed slope by slope
  • A witness coupon: sprayed alongside the roof and kept as physical evidence of what went on
  • Cured thickness: measured after cure and recorded on the homeowner’s certificate
  • Registered warranty: issued against that measurement, with the Roof Record behind it

03 The envelope math

Take your last replacement apart

Strip out the tear-off, the dumpster, the materials and most of the hours. Then run it with your own numbers.

Replacement job

  • Tear-off of the old roof
  • Dumpster, hauling and disposal
  • Underlayment, drip edge and new shingles
  • A roof open to the weather mid-job
  • Multi-day crew on site

NANOROOF preservation job

  • Roof-life assessment and written report
  • 10-point tune-up and repairs
  • Low-pressure soft wash
  • Baseline moisture readings
  • Spray application, according to the application protocol
  • Roof Record and warranty registration

A job with no tear-off and no new materials can be priced far below a replacement. That’s arithmetic, not a promise. We don’t quote margins, and you shouldn’t trust a number on a web page.

04 The inversion

The roofs you never get to bid

Advertise preservation, and the people who call are the ones who never called a roofer: aging roof, years left, not ready to replace.

  • Qualifies: you preserve it, with a written assessment and a registered warranty
  • Doesn’t qualify: you say so, in writing, with photos
  • Either way: you’re the roofer who didn’t sell them a roof they didn’t need. Who do they call when it’s time?

Where the work comes from

The situations that put an aging roof in front of a contractor, and the people who see them first.

Why a homeowner calls

  • Granules in the gutters and a roof that looks tired
  • Dark streaks and moss on the shaded slopes
  • Curling or cracking they can see from the driveway
  • An insurer or inspector flagged the roof’s age
  • Buying or selling, and the roof came up in the inspection
  • Solar going on, and the roof needs years left first
  • Not ready to replace, but not willing to ignore it

Who sends you work

  • Home inspectors who write “roof nearing end of life”
  • Real estate agents with a roof stalling a sale
  • Insurance agents whose clients face roof-age questions
  • Solar installers who need a sound roof first
  • Exterior cleaners and gutter crews on roofs every day
  • Property managers with many aging roofs
  • Painters and remodelers already working on the house
Expect to sign non-circumvention agreements. When a referral partner does some of the same work you do, you agree not to go after their customers for the work they do themselves, so a referral never costs them a client. Referral relationships and any compensation are yours to set up, and some professions, including home inspectors and real estate agents in many states, have rules about referral fees. Check yours first.

08 The honest limits

Who this is for

  • Owner-operators with crews, customers and a name in their market
  • Roofing companies that want work between replacements
  • Exterior-cleaning and gutter companies already on residential roofs
  • Solar installers who need the roof under the panels to have years left

Who it isn’t

  • Passive investors: if you don’t run a crew, there’s no version of this for you
  • Anyone selling a cure-all: soft decking, widespread damage and roofs at the end of their life get replaced
  • Anyone who wants earnings promises: we don’t make them

Questions contractors ask

Won’t this cannibalize my replacements?

The roofs that qualify weren’t going to be replaced this year anyway. The ones that don’t qualify are replacement conversations you’d never have been called for.

What if the roof leaks?

Leaks are repaired first, in the tune-up. Preservation isn’t a leak repair, and it’s never sold as one.

Can I apply in winter?
Do I need roofing experience?

Your crews need to work safely on roofs. Certification teaches the assessment, tune-up, soft wash and application protocol.

Will it work on every shingle roof?

A 25-point inspection scored out of 250, with ULEA Levels 1–7, determines whether the roof qualifies.

What is the opening product order?

An opening product order in the $25–30K range: one 269-gallon tote of SPS, a case of elastomeric caulk and a case of liquid flashing. Freight is extra and paid by the dealer.

Who’s behind NANOROOF?

Nano Products LLC in West Palm Beach, Florida, which supplies the professional-grade materials, the protocol and the warranty. About NANOROOF

How far does one tote go?

At the 10-year spec a tote covers about 43,000 sq ft before waste.

How much will I make?

We don’t make earnings claims. Take your last replacement, strip out the tear-off, disposal and materials, and run the job with your own numbers.

Do I need your software or marketing?

No. Both are optional. You can submit job data on paper, and you keep your own brand.
