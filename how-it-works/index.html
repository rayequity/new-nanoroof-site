Why shingles fail, and how NANOROOF slows it down

Asphalt shingles age through a loop of seven stages. NANOROOF works against five of them directly, by bonding inside the shingle and encapsulating it.

The failure loopThree ways to treat a shingleWhat's in NANOROOFWhat it won't do

Shingles don't wear out. They break down in a loop.

Every stage of shingle aging feeds the next, and the last stage exposes more asphalt to the sun, which starts it again. Tap a stage to see what happens and where NANOROOF interrupts it.

Three ways to treat an aging shingle

Surface coatings sit on the outside. Penetrating treatments soak in. NANOROOF does both: it penetrates and bonds inside the shingle and forms a protective layer on top, encapsulating it.

Surface coating

Forms a film over the granules. It never reaches the asphalt drying out underneath, and the film can crack and lift as the shingle moves.

Penetrating treatment

Soy-based rejuvenators and nano-silica penetrants soak into the weathered asphalt. Nothing stays on top of the granules, so the surface has no protective layer.

NANOROOF SPS

Penetrates and bonds inside the shingle, then forms a protective layer over the granules, encapsulating the shingle from the inside out.

What’s in the NANOROOF SPS System

What SPS means

A water-based silicone elastomer made with proprietary nano-chemistry, designed for asphalt shingles.

It penetrates the shingle, bonds to the shingle's structure, and encapsulates it, adding a hydrophobic protective layer against water and granule loss.

What NANOROOF won't do

Being clear about limits is part of why the warranty means something.

  • It doesn't fix leaks. Most leaks come from flashings around chimneys, skylights and walls. Those are repaired first, during the tune-up.
  • It doesn't save a failed roof. Soft decking, structural damage or shingles that have lost too many granules mean replacement.
  • It isn't for brand-new roofs. New shingles need to wear in before the material can bond.
  • It isn't a new roof. It preserves the one you have and comes with its own warranty.
