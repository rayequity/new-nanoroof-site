Three ways to treat an aging shingle

Surface coatings sit on the outside. Penetrating treatments soak in. NANOROOF does both: it penetrates and bonds inside the shingle and forms a protective layer on top, encapsulating it.

Surface coating

Forms a film over the granules. It never reaches the asphalt drying out underneath, and the film can crack and lift as the shingle moves.

Penetrating treatment

Soy-based rejuvenators and nano-silica penetrants soak into the weathered asphalt. Nothing stays on top of the granules, so the surface has no protective layer.

NANOROOF SPS

Penetrates and bonds inside the shingle, then forms a protective layer over the granules, encapsulating the shingle from the inside out.

  • The decking is soft or the roof has structural damage
  • Granule loss has gone too far for the shingles to be saved
  • The roof is so new that it doesn't need treatment yet

Age tells you when. Condition tells you how.

Where permits are pulled, a roof’s age is on record. Where they aren’t, it’s often an estimate. Either way, age only says when the roof went on, not how it’s holding up. Condition has been harder to prove, so it rarely counts. It can, once it’s measured, recorded and repeatable.

Treatments that soak in

Taken on faith

Once oils or penetrants soak into the asphalt, there is nothing left on the surface to check in the field. Whether the treatment is still working can’t be measured on the roof.
