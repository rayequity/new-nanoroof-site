Referral partners

Realtors, home inspectors, insurance agents and property managers meet aging roofs every week. Partner with us to give those owners an option besides replacement.

Illustration

How it works

  1. You introduce a property owner.
  2. The local dealer runs the assessment and keeps you informed.
  3. Your client gets a written answer and a Roof Record they can share.

Dealers agree not to go around partners on the work you bring them. Partner terms are shared on request.

Good fits

  • Realtors and listing agents
  • Home inspectors
  • Insurance agents
  • Property and HOA managers
  • Remodelers and gutter companies
Talk to us

Who we work with

People who see aging roofs before anyone else does.

Real estate agents

A documented Roof Record can be shared with buyers and inspectors.

Home inspectors

A qualified option to point to when a roof is aging but still sound, instead of only “replace soon.”

Insurance professionals

An alternative for owners whose roof is aging but who have no claim to file.

Solar installers

Confirm the roof under the panels has life left before they go on, and keep it that way.

Home improvement pros

Add a roof option for the clients you already serve, without becoming a roofer.

Property and HOA managers

One program across many buildings, with a report the board can act on.

How a referral works

  1. IntroduceSend us the owner’s name and address, with their permission.
  2. AssessThe local dealer books a free roof-life assessment.
  3. ReportThe owner gets a written assessment.
  4. Stay informedYou hear how it went, and dealers don’t go around you on the work you bring.

What your client gets

An honest answer they can use, whichever way it goes.

  • A free assessment: the whole roof, not just the shingles
  • A written report: qualifies or not, with photos
  • A Roof Record: shareable with buyers, inspectors and insurers
