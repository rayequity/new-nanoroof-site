The NANOROOF SPS System

SPS stands for Silicone Preservation System. It’s the professional-grade silicone treatment and everything around it that makes it work on a real roof.

See the products
Illustration

What the letters mean

S

Silicone

A water-based, recoatable silicone that bonds inside the shingle and encapsulates it.

Why silicone

P

Preservation

It protects a roof that’s still sound and keeps it in service. It isn’t a repair for a roof that has already failed.

Does my roof qualify?

S

System

Not a can of product. An assessment, repairs, cleaning, baseline readings, metered application and a registered record, done the same way on every roof.

What the SPS System isn’t

1Not a coating

It doesn’t sit on top of the granules as a film that can crack and peel. It bonds into the shingle surface.

2Not a rejuvenator

It doesn’t just soak oil into the asphalt and leave nothing on top. It also encapsulates the granules.

3Not a repair for a failed roof

Soft decking, open leaks and missing shingles are fixed first, or the roof isn’t a candidate.

Where it fits

  • Asphalt shingle roofs: that pass a roof-life assessment
  • Aging, not failed: fading, brittleness and early granule loss
  • Clean and repaired first: soft wash and tune-up come before treatment

Where it doesn’t

  • Roofs at the end of their life: replacement is the right call
  • Active leaks or soft decking: fixed first, or not a candidate
